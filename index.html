Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to the questions agency owners ask us most.

What is Hub دفتر? +

Hub دفتر is an agency operations platform for digital agencies and service providers. It replaces disconnected tools with one unified system covering attendance, leaves, projects, client passwords, time tracking, workload, and project profitability. Every plan includes eight working core modules plus three custom modules built for your agency.

Who is Hub دفتر designed for? +

Small digital agencies, software development agencies, web and mobile development teams, AI and digital service providers, and any service business that wants structured office and team management. It is built specifically for agencies, not as a generic task manager.

Which modules are available today? +

Eight core modules are available today: Business Analysis, Hub دفتر Overview, Attendance & Leave, Projects, Password Manager, Time Tracking, Workload & Capacity, and Employee Management. All eight are included in every plan. AI Copilot is a planned additional module currently in development.

What are the three custom modules? +

They are three additional modules built specifically for your agency — not three selected from the existing eight. They are designed around your office workflows and operational processes, based on a discovery meeting where we learn how your team works and where time is being spent.

Does every paying customer receive the three custom modules? +

Yes. Every paying customer receives all eight core modules plus three custom modules built for their agency, included in the plan price. There is no separate charge for the three custom modules.

How are the custom modules selected? +

After a discovery meeting. We learn your agency's workflow and how time is spent on your existing processes, then you and the Hub دفتر team decide together which three modules will make your operations more systematic.

When will the custom modules be delivered? +

During your first month. Your first week is reserved for onboarding; the three custom modules are agreed with you and delivered across the first month.

Are small future changes and maintenance included? +

Yes. Small changes up to 4 hours of work are free. Larger work and additional modules are quoted in development hours before work starts. Attendance or vault outages are resolved the same day; all other customer-reported issues within 2 working days.

How are additional new modules charged? +

The initial three custom modules are included for every paying customer. Small changes up to 4 hours of work are free. Larger work and additional modules are quoted in development hours before work starts.

Is AI Copilot available today? +

No. AI Copilot is a planned module currently in development. When released, it will be included in your plan with no extra subscription fee — your agency simply connects its own AI API key.

Are AI API usage costs included in the subscription? +

There is no extra subscription fee for AI Copilot. Your agency connects its own AI API key and pays only for what it uses directly with the AI provider.

What does each plan include? +

Every plan (Starter, Team Plan, Growth) includes all eight core modules, three custom modules built for your workflow, first-week onboarding, ongoing support, free small changes up to 4 hours of work, and same-day attendance/vault resolution (2 working days for other issues). The plans differ only by seat limit (10, 15, or 25 seats), not by features.

How are users counted? +

Every employee onboarded into your workspace counts as one user. A 10-seat Starter Plan lets you onboard 10 people; a 15-seat Team Plan lets you onboard 15 people. Within your plan's seat limit there is no separate per-seat charge — that is what "no seat penalties" means here.

Yes. Employee job titles are customizable — Developer, Designer, QA Engineer, Tech Lead, Project Manager, HR Manager, or any title your agency uses. If you need additional role or workflow customization, share your requirements and the Hub دفتر team will customize the setup for you.

What happens during the first-week onboarding? +

The first week is dedicated to onboarding. We explain the features, help your team understand how the software works, handle migration and integrations, and get everyone comfortable using the system. Your three custom modules are delivered during the first month, not the first week.

What support is available after onboarding? +

Ongoing support is included. Small changes up to 4 hours of work are free. Larger work and additional modules are quoted in development hours before work starts. Attendance or vault outages are resolved the same day; all other customer-reported issues within 2 working days. What happens to our agency data when we leave? +

When you leave, we export your agency's data upon request so you keep access to it, then permanently delete everything associated with your agency from our systems — projects, teams, employees, logs, and database records.

How is our data isolated and secured? +

Your agency receives a dedicated database instance and app deployment. Client passwords are stored in an AES-256 encrypted vault with an audit log recording who viewed or copied credentials and when.
