Who is Hub دفتر designed for? +

Small digital agencies, software development agencies, web and mobile development teams, AI and digital service providers, and any service business that wants structured office and team management. It is built specifically for agencies, not as a generic task manager.

They are three additional modules built specifically for your agency — not three selected from the existing eight. They are designed around your office workflows and operational processes, based on a discovery meeting where we learn how your team works and where time is being spent.

Yes. Every paying customer receives all eight core modules plus three custom modules built for their agency, included in the plan price. There is no separate charge for the three custom modules.

After a discovery meeting. We learn your agency's workflow and how time is spent on your existing processes, then you and the Hub دفتر team decide together which three modules will make your operations more systematic.

When will the custom modules be delivered? +

During your first month. Your first week is reserved for onboarding; the three custom modules are agreed with you and delivered across the first month.

Are small future changes and maintenance included? +

Yes. Small changes up to 4 hours of work are free. Larger work and additional modules are quoted in development hours before work starts. Attendance or vault outages are resolved the same day; all other customer-reported issues within 2 working days.

How are additional new modules charged? +

The initial three custom modules are included for every paying customer. Small changes up to 4 hours of work are free. Larger work and additional modules are quoted in development hours before work starts.

Every plan (Starter, Team Plan, Growth) includes all eight core modules, three custom modules built for your workflow, first-week onboarding, ongoing support, free small changes up to 4 hours of work, and same-day attendance/vault resolution (2 working days for other issues). Every employee onboarded into your workspace counts as one user. A 10-seat Starter Plan lets you onboard 10 people; a 15-seat Team Plan lets you onboard 15 people. Within your plan's seat limit there is no separate per-seat charge — that is what "no seat penalties" means here.
